Our story

We take care of the journey.

So children can focus on learning, growing, and enjoying their day.

From care to community

Transport with a personal touch

Lagoqoza School Transport is a small, community-based business founded by Faith Khulile Masombuka. Her work with a pre-school reflected a deep passion for children and their wellbeing. Expanding into transport was a natural next step.

Through word of mouth, the service has grown among parents who value punctuality, dependable vehicles, and drivers they can trust.

Our mission

Safe, reliable and affordable

To provide school transport that gives parents peace of mind and children a comfortable start and end to their school day.

Our vision

A trusted community partner

To become the most trusted school transport provider in the community, known for punctuality, safety, and genuine care for every learner.

Who we serve

Families and schools

We support parents and guardians looking for dependable daily transport, as well as schools seeking a trusted service to recommend to their families.
